# Red team: economic and incentive lens

Scope: the incentives behind the evidence and guidance in `evidence/sources.yaml`, `evidence/claims.yaml`, the shared policies and `tests/results/`. Checked 2026-10-19. An incentive is a reason to look harder at a claim. It is never proof that the claim is false. "Not found" means the item was searched for and not located. "Not retrieved" means a source exists but its full text could not be opened.

**Structural gap behind every row below:** none of the 46 entries in `sources.yaml` has a funding, sponsor or conflict-of-interest field. A grep for "fund", "conflict" or "sponsor" across `sources.yaml`, `claims.yaml` and `evidence-audit.md` finds nothing. Every finding here is therefore invisible to the register's own status scheme (`verified`, `partial-access` and so on).

## 1. Top 5 findings

1. **A digital RCT in the register was funded by the app's maker, and the register doesn't say so.** This is Baumeister et al. 2026 (SRC-RS-17, n=307), which feeds CLM-RS-DIGITAL-RCTS.
   - **Funding:** the Ärzteblatt full text says MiNDNET E-Health Solutions AG developed the app and funded the study ([article](https://di.aerzteblatt.de/int/archive/article/250938); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3238/arztebl.m2026.0065)).
   - **Authors' ties:** two authors, Karow and Lambert, hold MiNDNET shares and list a MiNDNET affiliation. A third, Schöttle, reports MiNDNET fees. The paper's own statement also says Karow and Lambert have "no conflict", which contradicts the ties listed above it.
   - **Kenter 2023 (SRC-RS-16):** two authors designed the intervention they tested. They declare no economic profit from it ([PMC10033990](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10033990/)).
   - **What the register does record:** the study was open-label, used an exploratory alpha of 10%, and had low adherence. It omits the developer funding.
2. **Most of the ADHD-specific CBT evidence comes from developer-led or allegiance-prone trials.**
   - **Solanto 2010 (SRC-RS-01):** tested a programme that the authors then published as a Guilford manual ([AJP reply letter](https://ajp.psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.2010.10101467r)).
   - **Safren 2010 (SRC-RS-02):** tested the programme published as *Mastering Your Adult ADHD* in Oxford's "Treatments That Work" series ([listing](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/es/solutions/ovid/mastering-your-adult-adhd--a-cognitive-behavioral-treatment-program--therapist-guide-14675)).
   - **Funding:** both trials were NIMH-funded (Europe PMC grant records). Their COI statements were not retrieved.
   - **Why it matters:** researcher allegiance correlates with outcome at about r=.26 across 30 meta-analyses ([Munder 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2013.02.002)).
   - **Implementation intentions:** the d=.65 figure (SRC-RS-19) comes from the method's originators. So does the 2024/25 update, which reports d=.27 to .66 across 642 tests and mentions no publication-bias test in its abstract ([KOPS record](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/handle/123456789/69905)).
   - **Effect on the repo:** modest. The repo already uses these as component or adjacent support, not as proof.
3. **The popular body-doubling claims come largely from commercial and community actors. The repo handles this well and should keep doing so.**
   - **Dopamine claim:** the "activates neurotransmitters" line in CHADD is attributed to Focusmate, a paid service, not to research (SRC-BD-10). Psych Central's dopamine line is an inference from a general review (SRC-BD-11).
   - **Most-quoted outcome figure:** "focus more than doubled" comes from a self-report survey with no control group. FLOWN, a body-doubling platform, commissioned it, and it was published as "Partner" content ([Neurodiversity in Business](https://neurodiversity.org/?p=11449)).
   - **Eagle 2024 survey:** recruited through TikTok and productivity subreddits, so its respondents were already converts. Its funding was not retrieved (PDF text not extractable).
   - **Verdict:** CLM-BD-MECHANISM is fair, not an overstatement. The FLOWN figure must never enter the register.
4. **The guidance to "search online for a crisis line" sends people into an ad-driven, uneven environment.**
   - **Search engines:** Google's suicide-prevention box appears inconsistently, depending on query, country and language ([Haim 2017](https://doi.org/10.1080/10410236.2015.1113484); [Scherr 2019](https://doi.org/10.1177/1461444818801010)).
   - **ADHD content online:** about half of the top #ADHD TikToks were misleading ([Yeung 2022](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437221082854)). Under 50% of claims matched DSM-5 ([Karasavva 2025, PMID 40106389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40106389/)).
   - **ADHD telehealth advertisers:** Done spent more than $40M on social ads and its founders were convicted ([NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/ceo-telehealth-company-done-charged-online-adderall-distribution-schem-rcna156986); [Fierce](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/founder-telehealth-startup-done-convicted-adderall-fraud-scheme)). Cerebral was ordered to pay $7M for sharing the health data of 3.2M people with advertisers ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/85135)).
   - **Effect:** the app-neutrality rule avoids endorsement, but it leaves the user facing a market that is actively advertising at them.
   - **Predatory crisis lines:** no peer-reviewed evidence was found for or against them.
   - **Recommendation:** in the urgent-but-not-immediate tier, allow two non-commercial directories already in the register, IASP (SRC-GD-15) and Find A Helpline (SRC-GD-14). A clinician should review this.
5. **The incentives on hosts and on the repo's own process both point towards overclaiming.**
   - **Host retention:**
     - Anthropic keeps chats for up to 5 years where the user allows training, against 30 days otherwise ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms)).
     - From May to September 2025, a court order required OpenAI to keep even chats users had deleted. Data already preserved stays held ([Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2025/06/openai-forced-to-preserve-chatgpt-chats); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/openai-no-longer-has-to-preserve-all-of-its-chatgpt-data-with-some-exceptions-192422093.html)).
   - **Host engagement:** OpenAI attributed its 2025 sycophancy rollback partly to over-weighting short-term thumbs-up feedback ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy/)).
   - **Effect on MPoT:** the "Deletion is real" heading is true only for files the suite controls. The policy text does admit this.
   - **The repo's own process:** the same model family wrote, ran and graded the tests (`tests/results/2026-10-19-simulated.md` says so). LLM judges favour their own outputs ([Panickssery 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076)). `clinician_reviewed` is false everywhere.
   - **Small inconsistencies:** 13 vs 14 skills, and 42 vs 43 claims. These suggest the release-ready framing is ahead of the checking.

## 3. What this lens does not show

- **No row shows a claim is false.** The developer-funded trial may still be accurate; it simply needs flagging.
- **Pharma ties in the guideline panels would bias towards medication.** The repo uses those guidelines only for non-drug scaffolding, so the bias runs largely orthogonal to how the repo uses them.
- **Not checked or not found:**
  - the COI statements for Solanto, Safren, Matsumoto, Liu 2026 and López-Pinar 2026;
  - the funding of Eagle 2024;
  - CHADD's current corporate sponsors;
  - any study of unofficial or predatory crisis lines.
